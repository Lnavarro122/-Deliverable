  • Visitor: Open from 9:00 am - 5:00 pm Monday - Saturday.
  • Parking: Free parking at the Willow Marsh, Northshore, and Creekway lots.
  • Directions: Follow park signs from Willow Road.

What shoes to bring

  • Hiking boots: best for rocky, muddy, or steep trails because of the ankle support.
  • Sneakers: good for paved or gravel sections.
  • Trail runners: lightweight, with good grip for well-kept dirt trails
  • Avoid: sandals, flip-flops, dress shoes on unpaved trails

Accessibility

Willow Marsh Boardwalk has entry ramps for wheelchairs and strollers. There are accessible bathrooms at the boardwalk as well as the Visitor Center. Old Mill Creek Trail is accessible for wheelchairs and strollers.
