Help, contact & hours

Report a park or trail problem

To report a fallen tree, lost & found trail items call the park office at 555-5555 or email us at trails@lakecity.example.

Visit us at the Visitors Center

Lake City Visitor Center is open from Monday through Sunday, 9:00 am - 5:00 pm

Volunteers help with park and trail trash clean up, community food drives, and helping out in the community garden. Sign up at the visitors center help desk.
