Help and contact

Report a park or trail problem

To report a fallen tree, lost & found trail items call the park office at 555-5555 or email us at trails@lakecity.example

In an emergency, call 911.

Contact us at the park office

  • Visitor Center desk: Monday - Saturday, 9:00 a.m - 5:00 p.m.
  • Phone: 555-5555.
  • Email: contact@lakecity.example

The Lake City Visitor Center desk answers questions about trials, hosting events, other inquiries.
